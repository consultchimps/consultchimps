# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community. Be
respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus discussion
on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, and
sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers will
review reports promptly, preserve confidentiality where practical, and take
proportionate action.
